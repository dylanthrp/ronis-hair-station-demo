Roni · Your stylist

A chair, a conversation, a cut you actually like.

I started Roni’s Hair Station because I wanted to do hair my way — with time for every client, the kind of attention you don’t get when someone’s rushing between three chairs. When you sit in my chair, I’m not watching the clock. I’m listening, asking questions, and figuring out what you actually want — even if you came in saying “just a trim.”

I work with the whole family: men, women, kids, teens, and anyone who’s been burned by a bad haircut and just wants their confidence back. Cuts, color, blowouts, and the kind of styling help that doesn’t require a 45-minute lecture on what products to buy.

My salon is inside The Hair Connection on Van Born Rd, so you get the friendly neighborhood feel of a one-chair station with the convenience of a full-service shop — parking right out front, easy to find, easy to get in and out.

What you can expect

  • Time to talk first. I ask what you want before I pick up the shears.
  • Honest pricing. No upsell, no surprise add-ons.
  • Family-friendly. Kids, grandparents, first haircuts — all welcome.
  • Work I stand behind. If something’s not right, tell me. I’ll fix it.

Services

A focused menu — everything I do well, nothing I don’t. Prices are starting points; the final price depends on hair length, density, and what we decide together.

Women’s cut & style

Wash, consultation, precision cut, and blow-dry finish. Long, short, curly, straight — I work with your hair, not against it.

from $35

from $22

Some of my best work isn’t the haircut — it’s the ten minutes before the haircut. The story about the new job, the kid who won’t sleep, the anniversary dinner, the parent who’s not doing well. You don’t have to share anything you don’t want to, but if you do, I’m here for it.

That’s the kind of salon I always wanted to find when I was the one in the chair. So that’s the one I built.
